Gunji Kyuma

Ginji Kyuma (久間欣治, Kyuuma Ginji) is a supporting character in Alice in Borderland. He is a Citizen who serves as King of Clubs in the Borderland. He is played by Tomohisa Yamashita. Kyuma is a tall, middle-aged man with a slightly muscular build. He has shoulder length black hair and brown eyes. In the show, he appears nude with no shirt or pants, which makes his teammates and the players uncomfortable.


In reality, he actually takes pride in presenting nude and claims that wearing clothes is a social construct. Kyuma claims that nudists have been around for many years and that clothes are just another way to oppress people. However, as seen in the game, Kyuma is actually a good leader. He treats his teammates with respect and distributes their points equally in the game.
