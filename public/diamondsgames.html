4 of Diamonds - Light Bulb

Four of Diamonds or Light Bulb is a game featured in Episode 5 of Alice in Borderland. In this game, players have to guess which switch (A, B, or C) controls the light bulb in the next room under a time limit. In the main room, three switches are on the wall and through the door is the room that contains a single light bulb.

The game venue is a closed room located in a reservoir. The time limit for this game is until the water level reaches the electrical wires.

King of Diamonds - Beauty Contest

This game is featured in Season 2, Episode 6 of Alice in Borderland. In this game, players must choose a number between 0 and 100. The winner is whoever picks the number closest to 80% of the group's average. While the winner stays safe, every othe player loses a point and risks being covered in sulfuric acid from a scale above their heads. As players get eliminated, the rules become more dangerous. For the first penalty, if 2 players choose the same number, they are both disqualified and lose a point. The second penalty is that when someone guesses the number exactly, everyone else loses 2 points. Lastly, for the third penalty, if one person chooses 0, the other player can automatically win by choosing 100.

The game takes place in a supreme court. The interior is a simple courtroom with large balancing scales filled with sulfuric acid behind each player.
