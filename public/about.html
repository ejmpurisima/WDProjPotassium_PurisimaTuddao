Arisu Ryohei

Ryōhei Arisu (有栖 良平) is the protagonist of Alice in Borderland. His speciality is Hearts, and he survives through empathy, logic, and emotional intelligence.

Mira Kano

Mira Kano (加納 未来) is the Queen of Hearts and a key antagonist. She manipulates players psychologically during her croquet game and is known for her calm yet sadistic demeanor.
